PCC CS-401 · B.Tech CSE, Semester 4

Computer Organization and Architecture

A working model of how a computer executes instructions — from number representation and arithmetic hardware, through processor control-unit design, to the memory hierarchy and an introduction to parallel/pipelined architectures.

12 teaching weeks 4 credits 3-0-2 L-T-P 7 / 12 lectures published
Course progress58%

Weeks 1–7 are published in full; weeks 8–12 are in progress. Each week links the slide deck (PDF) — supporting materials (notes, assignments, handouts, GATE practice) live alongside in the source repository.

Open materials. All decks are free to download and reuse. Slide decks are compiled from the Beamer source in the repository; derived prose notes, graded assignments, instructor handouts, and GATE practice sets mirror the same weekly numbering.

Prereq.Prerequisites

Digital Logic Design (or equivalent: Boolean algebra, combinational/sequential circuits, flip-flops, basic ALU/adder circuits). Basic programming/algorithmic literacy is assumed but not tested directly.
