PCC CS-401 · B.Tech CSE, Semester 4

Computer Organization and Architecture

A working model of how a computer executes instructions — from number representation to pipelined, parallel machines.

12 teaching weeks 4 credits 3-0-2 L-T-P 7 / 12 lectures published
Browse the weekly lectures
Course progress — weeks 1–7 published in full 58%

Open materials. Every week ships four freely reusable PDFs: the slide deck, the prose lecture notes, the graded assignment, and a GATE practice set with answers. Solution keys to assignments are never published.

§ 00 · Before you begin

Prerequisites

Digital Logic Design (or equivalent: Boolean algebra, combinational/sequential circuits, flip-flops, basic ALU/adder circuits). Basic programming/algorithmic literacy is assumed but not tested directly.
