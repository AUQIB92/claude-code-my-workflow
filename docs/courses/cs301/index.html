§ 00 · Before you begin

Prerequisites

Programming in C (functions, arrays, struct, recursion, basic file I/O). Basic discrete mathematics — sets, logarithms, summations, and proof by induction — is assumed for the complexity analysis from Week 2 onward. No prior exposure to data structures is assumed.

Foundations, Pointers & ADTs

Structured programming; pointers and dynamic memory management in C (malloc/free); the stack and the heap; the abstract data type.

Anchor readings
  • Wirth §4.2, p.111; Sedgewick & Wayne §1.2–1.4, pp.64/120/172; Karumanchi Ch. 1, p.18
Week 02 In progress

Algorithm Analysis

Space and time complexity; asymptotic notation and orders of growth; best-, worst-, and average-case efficiency.

Anchor readings
  • Horowitz & Sahni Ch. 1; Neapolitan & Naimipour Ch. 1–2
Week 03 In progress

Arrays & Stacks

1D/2D array address arithmetic; the Stack ADT and its array implementation; balanced-parenthesis checking, postfix evaluation.

Anchor readings
  • Horowitz & Sahni Ch. 2–3; Aho–Hopcroft–Ullman Ch. 2
Week 04 In progress

Expression Evaluation & Recursion

Infix/postfix/prefix conversion; Tower of Hanoi; recursion and the runtime stack.

Anchor readings
  • Horowitz & Sahni Ch. 3; Aho–Hopcroft–Ullman Ch. 2
Week 05 In progress

Queues

Circular queues, double-ended queues; priority queues over arrays.

Anchor readings
  • Horowitz & Sahni Ch. 3; Aho–Hopcroft–Ullman Ch. 2
Week 06 In progress

Singly Linked Lists

Linked vs. array representation; insertion, deletion, search, display, and reverse in singly linked lists.

Anchor readings
  • Horowitz & Sahni Ch. 4; Aho–Hopcroft–Ullman Ch. 2
Week 07 In progress

Doubly Linked Lists & Applications

Doubly and circular linked lists; stack/queue over a linked list; polynomial manipulation.

Anchor readings
  • Horowitz & Sahni Ch. 4; Aho–Hopcroft–Ullman Ch. 2
Week 08 In progress

Searching & Elementary Sorting

Linear and binary search; insertion, selection, bubble, and counting sort with their analysis.

Anchor readings
  • Horowitz & Sahni Ch. 7; Neapolitan & Naimipour Ch. 2
Week 09 In progress

Efficient Sorting

Quick, merge, shell, radix, and heap sort; the comparison-sort lower bound.

Anchor readings
  • Horowitz & Sahni Ch. 7; Aho–Hopcroft–Ullman Ch. 8
Week 10 In progress

File Organization & Hashing

Sequential, direct, indexed-sequential, and hashed file organization; open and closed hashing.

Anchor readings
  • Horowitz & Sahni Ch. 8; Aho–Hopcroft–Ullman Ch. 4
Week 11 In progress

Trees, BSTs & AVL

Binary tree traversals and expression trees; BST insertion/deletion/min/max; AVL rotations.

Anchor readings
  • Horowitz & Sahni Ch. 5; Aho–Hopcroft–Ullman Ch. 3, 5
Week 12 In progress

Graphs: Traversal, MST, Shortest Paths

Adjacency list/matrix; BFS/DFS; Prim’s and Kruskal’s MST algorithms; Dijkstra’s shortest path.

Anchor readings
  • Horowitz & Sahni Ch. 6; Aho–Hopcroft–Ullman Ch. 6–7
