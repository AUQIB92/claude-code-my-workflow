## Cross-check finding (2026-08-10) — corrects an existing citation error across the whole syllabus

**This edition's Chapter 9 is "Embedded Systems," not arithmetic.** `syllabi/CS401.md`'s Week 2 row (and every other week that cites "Hamacher Ch. 9") assumed Ch. 9 covers number representation/arithmetic — this was an unverified, pre-indexing citation (per `.claude/rules/textbook-grounding.md`'s "standard treatment, not page-verified" status). Direct inspection of this PDF's table of contents (PDF pages 7-13, OCR'd) and the Chapter 6/9 title pages shows:

- **Chapter 6 ("ARITHMETIC," p.367-410)** is the actual home of addition/subtraction hardware, fast adders, multiplication (including the Booth algorithm), fast multiplication, integer division (restoring/non-restoring), and floating-point — i.e., everything Weeks 2-3 need.
- **Chapter 9 ("Embedded Systems," p.511+)** covers microcontrollers, polling/interrupt I/O programming, and timing constraints — relevant to **Week 8 (I/O techniques)**, not Weeks 2-3.
- Sec. 2.1 (p.27-33) of **Chapter 2** does carry an introductory treatment of number representation, signed addition/subtraction, and overflow, which does back the syllabus's "number systems and representation" phrase for Week 2 — so the existing "Hamacher Ch. 2" half of the citation was correct; only the "Ch. 9" half was wrong.

**Fixed in this pass:** `syllabi/CS401.md`'s Week 2 topic/readings rows and `Slides/CS401/02-number-systems-arithmetic.tex`'s Booth's-algorithm and Division citations now read "Hamacher Ch. 6" instead of "Ch. 9."

**Not yet fixed (flagged for follow-up, out of this session's scope):** every other week that cites "Hamacher Ch. 9" or an unverified Hamacher chapter number likely needs the same correction once those chapters are indexed — in particular:
- Week 3 (IEEE754) cites "Hamacher Ch. 9" — should almost certainly be **Ch. 6 Sec. 6.7** (Floating-Point Numbers and Operations, p.393+, immediately following the range indexed here).
- Weeks 4-7 cite "Hamacher Ch. 7" for registers/instructions/control — this book's Ch. 7 is "Basic Processing Unit" (execution, hardwired control §7.4, microprogrammed control §7.5), which plausibly backs Weeks 6-7 (hardwired/microprogrammed control) but not Week 4 (register/instruction formats, which live in Ch. 2) or Week 5 (addressing modes, which are Ch. 2 Sec. 2.5, not Ch. 7 — though Ch. 7's Sec. 7.3 "Multiple-Bus Organization" p.423 may back Week 5's bus-organization content specifically). Needs a real indexing pass to resolve, not inference from the preface.
- Weeks 9-10 cite "Hamacher Ch. 8" for cache/virtual memory — this book's Ch. 8 is "Pipelining." The preface indicates caches/memory are **Chapter 5** instead.
- Week 11 cites "Hamacher Ch. 5, 10" for parallel/pipelining — this book's Ch. 8 is Pipelining and Ch. 10 is (per the preface) peripheral devices/computer interconnections, not parallel processing.

This is the same class of issue as the already-flagged Stallings2015 edition mismatch (`knowledge-base-CS401.md`) — a citation written before the book was indexed, now shown to be wrong by direct inspection. Recommend a full `/index-textbook CS401/Hamacher2002` pass (indexing Ch. 1, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10 in addition to what's here) before trusting any "Hamacher Ch. N" citation outside Weeks 1-2 at page-verified confidence.
